ESAC Registry of Open Publishing Agreements

 

The ESAC Registry collects and presents essential information about open access publishing agreements worldwide. By documenting key terms, conditions and service components, the Registry helps institutions, consortia and publishers better understand the evolving landscape of OA agreements, compare documented results across contexts, and strengthen shared understanding.

The ESAC Registry is available here in two formats: as a searchable table and as an interactive matrix visualization. Use the tabs below to switch between the two views.
