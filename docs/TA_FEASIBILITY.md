## Feasibility judgment for Paper 1

- Window is short (2023 pilot → 2024–2025 data; 2–3 post years against a 20-year panel).
- Institution-level OA share shift in eligible Springer/Nature hybrid sources is measurable with OpenAlex institution×source×year counts — a compact event-check is feasible and methodologically defensible as *descriptive mechanism evidence* (not causal).
- Verdict: include as a **bounded mechanism check** only if the institution list can be reconstructed reliably; otherwise report feasibility and defer to Paper 2. Decision logged in DECISIONS.md.
